# Japanese Quiz Game

A timed arcade-style quiz over JLPT N3 and N4 vocabulary and grammar, built as a 1980s Japanese
game-center cabinet.

## Language

**Members Roll**:
The screen, reached from the title screen's CREDIT FREE PLAY readout, where each team member's name, role, and
the things they contributed scroll past in an endless loop, like a film's closing credits.
_Avoid_: Staff roll, credits, credits page, credit scene, about page
